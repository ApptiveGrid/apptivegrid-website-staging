AI for Business

Controlled, not random

Many companies are already experimenting with AI. The challenge is integrating AI safely and reliably into existing processes. With ApptiveGrid, AI becomes part of a structured workflow.

  • AI works based on your data and processes.
  • Results can be reviewed, approved, or further processed.
  • Workflows remain transparent and controllable.
  • AI steps are combined with fixed rules and business logic.
  • Different AI services can be integrated flexibly.

The result is a controlled, deterministic use of AI that integrates into existing business processes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need programming skills?

No. ApptiveGrid is a no-code platform. You create databases, forms, portals, workflows, and AI-powered processes visually.

Can I start with a template?

Yes. You can use ready-made templates and customize them to your requirements.

Can external users submit data?

Yes. Forms can be shared via link, QR code, or website embedding.

Can ApptiveGrid connect to existing tools?

Yes. ApptiveGrid can be connected to other systems via integrations, APIs, webhooks, and automations.

Can I use different AI services?

Yes. ApptiveGrid can serve as a central platform for integrating different AI services into your processes and connecting them with your business data.

How is AI used in a controlled way?

AI is used as part of defined workflows. Business rules, approvals, data structures, and automations ensure that results remain traceable and controllable.

Is ApptiveGrid GDPR-compliant?

Yes. ApptiveGrid is focused on data protection and is developed and hosted in Germany.
