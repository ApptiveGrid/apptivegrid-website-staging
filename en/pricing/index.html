Frequently Asked Questions

Can I try the Pro plan for free?

Yes! Every new account gets a free 4-week trial of the Pro plan – no credit card required.

Are form fillers charged?

No. Persons who only fill out forms do not count as users and incur no costs.

Are there special terms for startups or NGOs?

Yes, we offer special programs for startups and non-profit organizations. Contact us for more information.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored and processed exclusively in Germany – fully GDPR-compliant.
