The team behind ApptiveGrid

We are a team from Cologne with a mission: to make digitization accessible to everyone – without any coding.

Our Mission

Digitization without barriers

ApptiveGrid was founded because we saw how many companies struggle with digitization – not because of missing ideas, but because of missing developers, high costs, and complex systems.

With ApptiveGrid we give companies the tools to develop their own digital solutions – without IT knowledge, without programmers, without months of development time.

Technology

The best products emerge when everything works together perfectly.

That is why we build ApptiveGrid on a technology that we understand and control right down to its core.

With Pharo we use not just a programming language, but a fully integrated development platform. With SOIL we built our own database on top of it. Together they form the foundation of ApptiveGrid.

By developing the core building blocks of our platform ourselves, we lay the groundwork for long-term independence, maximum control and continuous innovation.

“Why? Because we are convinced that digital sovereignty begins with technological independence. Companies should not have to rely on their most important systems, data and innovations being controlled by a handful of global technology providers.”

Europe should not merely use digital products. Europe should own the technologies they are built on.

The result is more than just software: European technology, developed in Europe, operated according to European values and created for organisations that want to shape their own digital future.
